Bull Rush Privacy Policy

FRONT PAGE studios · Last updated 31 October 2026

Bull Rush is a stock market board game made by FRONT PAGE studios. We wrote this page to explain, in plain words, what happens to your information when you play. The short version: we do not collect it.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email or phone number.
  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No real money. All trading in the game uses play money, and all companies and news are fictional.

What stays on your device

Your player name, avatar, settings, saved game, career progress and stats are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Online multiplayer

When you choose to play "Same Wi-Fi" or "Anywhere" multiplayer, the game sends game moves, your chosen player name, avatar and quick-chat messages to the other players in your room. To connect players, these messages pass through public connection services (the PeerJS signalling server and public MQTT relay brokers such as EMQX, HiveMQ and Eclipse Mosquitto). These services only pass the messages along while the room is open. We do not run them, and we do not store or read the messages. Rooms use a short random code, so only people with the code can join. Do not put personal information in your player name.

Pass & Play and games against bots work fully offline and send nothing over the internet.

Spoken quick-chat

Quick-chat phrases are spoken using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The text is processed on your device by that engine.

Children

Bull Rush is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Write to us at the developer email shown on the Bull Rush page in Google Play.
